# Cognifyz Data Analysis Internship Projects

This repository contains the completed work for the **Cognifyz Technologies – Data Analysis Internship**.  
The project focuses on analyzing a restaurant dataset to extract meaningful insights related to cuisines, city-wise distribution, pricing patterns, ratings, online delivery, and geographic trends.

- **Organization:** Cognifyz Technologies

## 📊 Level 1 – Exploratory Data Analysis

### Tasks Performed

**1. Top Cuisines Analysis**
- Identified the top three most common cuisines
- Calculated the percentage of restaurants serving each cuisine

**2. City Analysis**
- Determined the city with the highest number of restaurants
- Calculated average ratings for each city
- Identified the city with the highest average rating

**3. Price Range Distribution**
- Analyzed restaurant distribution across different price ranges
- Calculated percentage share for each price category

**4. Online Delivery Analysis**
- Calculated the percentage of restaurants offering online delivery
- Compared average ratings of restaurants with and without online delivery

### Outcome  
Level 1 analysis provides insights into customer preferences, affordability trends, and the influence of service convenience on restaurant ratings.

---

## 📈 Level 2 – Advanced Data Analysis

### Tasks Performed

**1. Restaurant Ratings Analysis**
- Studied the distribution of aggregate ratings
- Identified the most common rating range
- Calculated the average number of votes received

**2. Cuisine Combination Analysis**
- Identified the most common cuisine combinations
- Analyzed rating trends across different cuisine pairings

**3. Geographic Analysis**
- Visualized restaurant locations using latitude and longitude
- Identified geographic clustering in major regions

**4. Restaurant Chain Analysis**
- Identified restaurant chains present in the dataset
- Analyzed ratings and popularity of different chains

### Outcome  
Level 2 focuses on deeper analytical insights, highlighting relationships between cuisine combinations, geography, brand presence, and customer engagement.
